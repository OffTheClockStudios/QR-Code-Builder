1. Paste shipment report

The Quantity column is available inventory. Date columns are daily shipments. Parenthesized shortages are informative only; this tool recalculates cumulative shortages from quantities and shipments. On a fresh report import, choose whether to replace or carry forward completed production to avoid double-counting.

2. Run priority & lot plan

Use ↑/↓ to set your own sequence. Planned lots are future lots you intend to run; use Complete 1 lot as each lot finishes to add pieces to available stock and subtract one remaining planned lot. Use +/− to adjust planned remaining lots. Changes recheck all shipment dates.

A run order is a planning aid, not proof shipments can be met: production capacity, changeovers, inspection/release status, and pickup cutoffs are not modeled. Overdue dates are sorted first. Confirm inventory and lot conventions with your plant's system.

3. Shipment coverage by part and date

Projected balances include available inventory, completed lots entered here, and remaining planned lots. Red negative balances indicate a shortage even if all remaining planned lots finish before that shipment. This is a coverage check, not a time-phased schedule.
