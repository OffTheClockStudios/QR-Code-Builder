How are parts needed and lots calculated?

Each date cell is treated as daily demand; only its first number is used. A parenthesized number such as 123 (-30) is an existing projected-shortage note and is not counted again. We add daily demand through the selected cutoff, subtract the row's Quantity (on-hand supply), then subtract completed pieces credited here. Parts still needed = max(0, demand − Quantity − completed pieces). Lots needed = round up(parts still needed ÷ current lot size). Planned lots reduce the after plan shortage, but they do not reduce parts still needed until you click Complete 1 lot. That action moves one planned lot to completed, crediting the current lot size; Undo reverses it. The app remembers each finished lot's size even if you change the lot size later. Important: if you paste a new report that already includes completed production in Quantity, updating the report clears this page's completion history to avoid double counting. The Min and Max columns are not used. Dates are evaluated in the order they appear in your pasted report.
