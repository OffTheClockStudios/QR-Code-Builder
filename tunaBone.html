Use the font menu and font-size slider to apply one adjustable font across the entire page and diagram. The other sliders adjust branch length, main bubble size (branches and effect), and child-item bubble size; diagram text also scales with each bubble setting. Click a branch or item bubble to edit it. Drag any bubble to reposition it; positions are saved automatically. Click directly on a branch line to add a child item at that point. The new bubble appears to the left, and its connector remains anchored to the exact point you clicked. Right-click any bubble to cycle: Unknown → Good → Bad → Suspect.
